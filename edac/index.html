How it works

  1. Brine loop. A 1 m³ tank holds the saline working solution. A pump feeds it to the electrolyzer.
  2. Electrolyzer. DC power splits the brine into an acid stream and a base stream, and co-produces hydrogen and oxygen. It runs on intermittent renewable power.
  3. Air contactors. Fans pull air through packing wetted with the base. CO₂ reacts with the base and stays in the liquid as carbonate. CO₂-lean air leaves through the top.
  4. CO₂ release. The carbonate-rich liquid meets the acid in the release vessel. The pH swings back down and CO₂ comes off as a concentrated gas.
  5. Loop closes. Neutralised brine returns to the tank. The control cabinet sequences pumps, fans and stack current.
